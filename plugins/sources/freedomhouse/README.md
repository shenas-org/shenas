# Freedom House (`shenas-source-freedomhouse`)

Freedom House *Freedom in the World* country scores for Shenas.

## Basis

This plugin is part of Shenas's source-plugin cohort. It ships under the [Shenas inclusion policy for source plugins](https://shenas.org/policy/sources) because Freedom House publishes its annual reports and scores for public consumption and permits redistribution with citation. Terms reference: [Freedom House content permissions](https://freedomhouse.org/about-us/content-permissions). Scope: this plugin reads Freedom House *Freedom in the World* country scores published by Freedom House on behalf of the user; no data is sent off-device by the plugin.

## Attribution

Source: Freedom House, *Freedom in the World*. Reproduced with citation in accordance with Freedom House's content permissions policy.
